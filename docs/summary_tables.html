Cabrillo National Monument

Native Bee Summary Tables 🐝

by Brandi Sanchez

A by-the-numbers look at the native bees of Cabrillo National Monument. How many native bees have been found, and the flowers they visit. So far: 31 bee genera, 78 species, and 2 subspecies, recorded on 184 plant genera. Everything here is a plain count you can look up and cite, with no statistics or interpretation, just what was recorded in the field.
